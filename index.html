Case 01

Femmes

Naming · Brand voice · Content strategy · Verbal identity

The challenge

Creating the verbal identity of a medical center specialized in gynecology and menopause for upper-middle-class women in Mexico City. The real challenge wasn't just naming a center — it was claiming menopause as a stage that deserves specialized attention, with a premium and human tone at the same time. Existing references in CDMX operated in opposite registers — clinically cold or warmly generic — with no real differentiation or specialization in menopause.

Strategic approach

A semantic reference universe was built using nine root words across four languages — Nahuatl, Maya, Latin and Greek — linked to the center's values: luna, mujer, ciclo, alma, integralidad, femineidad. From there, 30+ candidates across five territories were explored, each developed with DNA, taglines and real usage contexts. The shortlist of six names was validated with the creative director and chosen by the client in a single session.

System thinking

The brand's communication was defined across every touchpoint — from the first ad to post-consultation follow-up. Tone, language and intent stay consistent throughout: clinic, web, social media, email, WhatsApp, Google Ads. The approach established how Femmes communicates at every stage of the patient's journey.

What I built

Naming

Final name with DNA, etymology and cultural justification. Glossary of 9 root keywords with cultural context.

Voice & tone system

4 tone pillars with visual tone scale, real usage examples and counter-examples for each.

Messaging principles

Primary payoff + campaign line + 4 secondary support phrases for the verbal system.

Content guidelines

Style guide, language intent and avoidables list. Channel-by-channel application with description and examples.

"The key shift was moving from describing the center to speaking from a medical philosophy: understand before treating. Femmes doesn't convince — it declares what it believes."

Case 02

Is My Lova

Brand strategy · Verbal identity

The challenge

IS MY LOVA is a fashion and art boutique based in Miami, founded by women from different parts of the world with a shared vision: to redefine what a luxury retail experience looks and feels like. The challenge was building a complete brand strategy and verbal identity for a space that needed to hold two things at once — the authority of a luxury fashion house and the eclecticism of a concept store deeply rooted in Miami's cultural energy. The brand needed a clear strategic foundation before anything visual could take shape.

"IS MY LOVA doesn't sell fashion. It curates identity." — The single premise from which the entire brand strategy was built.

Strategic approach

The process began with that central insight. From there, the full brand strategy was developed — defining purpose, positioning, audience, pillars, payoff and narrative. Every decision was built around one central concept: presence. Not as an aesthetic quality, but as something a woman carries when she knows exactly who she is and what she's wearing.

Verbal identity

The verbal identity established how the brand speaks — direct, editorial, precise — and drew a clear line between what IS MY LOVA says about itself and what it simply does. Including a deliberate framework for how its social purpose is expressed: through action and curatorial decisions, not as a marketing declaration.

Brand pillars

Curation as Authority Presence Intimate Luxury Culture Identity

What I built

Brand strategy

Purpose, value proposition, mission, vision, key differentiators and brand essence.

Brand narrative

Storytelling framework connecting the word Lova, multicultural founding and Miami's identity into a coherent brand story.

Tone of voice

Archetype, personality traits, tone scale, language guidelines and application examples across in-store, website and social media.

Communication pillars

Five pillars with core messages and copy direction for consistent brand communication.

Positioning

Category definition, target audience — demographic and psychographic — positioning statement and differentiation framework.

Social purpose framework

Strategic recommendation for how IS MY LOVA's social commitments live separately from its core brand narrative.

"The clearest decision in the project was also the most delicate: keeping the brand's social purpose out of its central narrative — not to diminish it, but to protect it."

System thinking

Every written element was built to function as a system. The narrative connects to the pillars. The pillars inform the tone. The tone shapes the copy. Nothing was written in isolation.

Case 03

L'Topia

Communication strategy · Content strategy · Audience segmentation · Brand narrative · Editorial direction

The challenge

L'TOPIA is a luxury residential development within The Awen in Tulum — designed to redefine high-end living in the Riviera Maya through timeless architecture, sustainable technology and a conscious lifestyle. The challenge was strategic and narrative in equal parts: launching a new brand with no digital presence, no landing page, and no established positioning — in a market visually saturated with boho-wellness aesthetics — while communicating to three fundamentally different audiences with distinct emotional triggers, investment mindsets and definitions of luxury.

Strategic approach

I started with a competitive audit of eight reference brands — including Faena, Serhant, Pelican Hill, Trovbu and James Edition — mapping what to absorb and what to actively avoid. The diagnosis was clear: most competitors relied on generic aspirational copy, excessive wellness codes, or cold financial language. None had built a differentiated narrative system.

What I built

From that foundation, I built the full communication architecture: a master narrative, three audience-specific narratives, a six-phase rollout strategy, a content pillar system, and channel-specific guidelines — all grounded in a single central idea.

"L'TOPIA isn't bought. It's belonged to." — The line that anchored every strategic and creative decision in the project.

Audience architecture

One of the core strategic decisions was building three distinct narrative layers for three audiences with different relationships to the brand — each with its own tone, messaging triggers and conversion logic.

Audience A

Loyal investors

Existing clients from The Awen. Motivated by ROI, legacy and portfolio continuity. Tone: direct, confident, data-informed.

Audience B

Lifestyle aspirationals

New buyers driven by identity and belonging. Tone: poetic, evocative, intimate — "country club meets jungle."

Audience C

Brokers & ambassadors

Strategic partners seeking status and exclusive access. Tone: institutional, selective, club-like — "by invitation only."

Deliverables

Master narrative

Central brand concept, brand purpose, tone guidelines and key messaging pillars. Defined what L'TOPIA says — and what it never says.

Audience narratives

Three complete narrative systems — each with its own insight, tone, strategy, content formats and copy examples.

Content system

Five content pillars — lifestyle, product, backstage, educational and community — with formats and examples per channel and per phase.

Channel strategy

Platform-specific guidelines for Instagram, Facebook, email, Meta Ads, landing page and PR — each with funnel role, tone and tactics.

6-phase rollout

From strategy and planning (Phase 0) through official launch and PR (Phase 5) — each phase with its own narrative, tactics and KPIs.

Measurement framework

Global and per-channel KPIs, reporting cadence, accountability plan and tool stack — from Meta Business Suite to CRM and Google Analytics.

Channel strategy

Instagram & Facebook
Awareness + aspiration. Cinematic reels, editorial carousels, sensory stories. Old money tone, no boho clichés.
Email marketing
Trust + nurture + conversion. Segmented sequences per audience. "By invitation only" for A and C. Aspirational storytelling for B.
Meta Ads
Qualified traffic generation. Engagement in Phase 1, conversion in Phase 3, dynamic remarketing in between.
Landing page
Central conversion point. Segmented forms, A/B testing, villa gallery and clear download CTA for the exclusive catalog.
PR & events
Prestige and credibility. Press kit, luxury media placements and private preview events for brokers and top investors.

Phased execution

Phase 0
Strategy & planning
Kick-off, benchmarking, buyer personas, narrative system, CRM setup.
Phase 1
Lifestyle awareness
"Not just architecture — it's belonging to a truly selective community."
Phase 2
Expectation + storytelling
"Be part of the Founder's Circle." Brand humanization through behind-the-scenes content.
Phase 3
Catalog + conversion
"By invitation only." Catalog downloads, 1:1 meetings, broker event.
Phase 4
Community + VIP
"Being part of L'TOPIA is a way of life." Private events, lifestyle series.
Phase 5
Official launch + PR
"A legacy you inhabit." Media coverage, testimonials, final conversions.

System thinking

The strategy was built to work as an integrated system — where the master narrative defines what the brand believes, the audience narratives translate that into specific emotional languages, and the channel strategy determines how each message reaches each person at the right moment in their journey.

Central narrative L'TOPIA isn't bought. It's belonged to.
